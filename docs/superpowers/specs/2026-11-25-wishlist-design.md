# Wishlist 2026-11-25 (second batch): design and plan

Base: v0.216.1. The user answered eight questions, then asked for everything to be built overnight.
Lines marked **(call)** were decided by Claude without asking.

## Approaches
1. **Feature branches built by parallel agents, merged one at a time (picked).** Isolated worktrees, at most three at
   once (RAM). Same method as earlier wishlists. Costs merge conflicts in lang, advancements and registry notes.
2. One agent in sequence on main: no conflicts, but far too slow for this list.

No new libraries (the quest screen is our own, not FTB Quests). Not in scope: quest rewards, taming predators,
electrum coins or tools, restarting the play server. Delivery: tested, merged, pushed to main.

## Work packages
### 1. vehfix (wave 1)
- A hitched horse turns at a limited rate, slowest when standing, so the wagon swings at its own pace. Hitched speed is capped.
- Two players dragging one carcass: capped speed, damped turning, tether for every multi-hauler drag. No spinning or flying.
- Pig, Cow, Wild Boar, Red Deer and everything heavier can only be dragged: no shoulder carry, not hung on a horse.
  Sheep, Goat, Mouflon and Roe Deer stay carriable. **(call)** other species follow their bulk against these.
- Wagon and cart chests open with anything in the hand (placing modules and loading a hauled load still win).
- The chest opened is the one aimed at (per-cell ray test, also used for punching modules off).
- Riding a horse up to a wagon shows an "attach" prompt, and the harness key works from the saddle.

### 2. husbandry (wave 1)
- Joined Feeding Troughs share one water pool and show one level; one bucket adds what it adds today. **(call)**
  The same pooling for food if it has the same visual fault.
- Breeding is natural: livestock and tamed animals breed by themselves when they are content, which needs a Feeding
  Trough in range with food and water, plus an adult partner. Hand-feeding no longer starts breeding.
  **(call)** cooldown, local population cap, no winter births (as today).
- Taming of farm-friendly wild animals with a favourite food: Wild Boar, Mouflon, Aurochs, Reindeer, the deer
  species, Moose and Hare, plus others of the same kind. No predators. Tamed animals stop fleeing, follow their food,
  stay (never despawn), can be fenced, have husbandry needs and breed as above. Existing horse/onager/camel/fennec
  taming stays.

### 3. guistyle (wave 1)
- Every vanilla screen gets the hide/bark panel style of the caravan screen: inventory, creative, all containers,
  buttons, sliders, text fields, pause, title, options and other menus. Mostly texture overrides under
  `assets/minecraft/textures/gui/`, generated by a script.
- The panel drawing of the caravan screen becomes a shared helper class `com.mechanical.client.ui.HideUi`.
- Trade screen upgraded. Vanilla villagers use it too instead of the vanilla merchant screen.

### 4. questscreen (wave 2, after guistyle)
- The Advancements screen is replaced by a quest screen like FTB Quests: chapter list (today's tabs), a draggable and
  zoomable quest graph with dependency lines, and a detail pane with title, what to do, progress and what it unlocks.
- Advancements stay as the backend (about 890 entries, progress kept). No rewards.

### 5. metalmagma (wave 2)
- High Furnace already supports alloys. Add **Electrum**: Gold 1 : Silver 1, about 1100 °C; ingot, nugget, block.
- Electrum Ring and Necklace, **(call)** one step above silver and below gold if the numbers leave room, else equal
  to gold with something of its own.
- Magma Block: warms its surroundings (also against depth cold); works under the Baking Stone, Kiln and Smelting
  Furnace as a heat that never runs out but is weaker than coal (slower, no hot melts); can be crushed into a material
  for refractory blocks and to light the High Furnace.
- Nether and End disabled for now: no travel there, no new portals, no eyes into End frames. Getting home still works.
  One switch turns it back on.

### 6. world (wave 2)
- Better-looking acacia in worldgen and from saplings. **(call)** a custom tree placer: leaning forked trunk, flat
  layered umbrella crowns at different heights. Existing trees stay.
- Web map: grass, foliage and water are tinted per biome (today every biome has the same colour).
- Vanilla villages and the Hermit's Hut get names and show "now entering".

### 7. capes (wave 3)
- Elders, Trader Caravan leaders and the Oak Keeper wear a swaying cape with shoulder pieces, like a RuneScape skill
  cape, each in its own style (elder: tribe dye and carved bone/fur; trader: rich travelling cloth with brass clasps;
  Oak Keeper: moss, oak leaves and bark).

## Rules for every package
Advancements for new things, registry notes (sv + en), age docs, a gametest per new piece of logic (`TestArea.of`),
English item names. Visual work is checked in a muted client with screenshots. The version is bumped at merge.
